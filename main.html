Preface
Since the dusk of life, I've always been a challenger of nature. I have broke through how far I can push myself against the force of nature many and many times, from attempting to climb the highest mountains to going to the driest place and see how long I can survive.
But one thing captures me during the expeditions - Snow.
Powdery, yet solid and common. Something that seems right ripped off from a delicate chemistry experiment, but from what it seems like, nature can provide the same.
And now I look for more. More power to alter the nature, to experience what a bigger challenge may be.

And one day, seems like the godness had grant me the potential to do so.
Faith that fills my heart with a container of power, currently devoid of what I wish.
And the main objective of my curious and adventurous life - is to see nature at its fullest.
Time to gather what we need.
01
The power grows. Slowly but steadily, I can start feeling the nature turning around me as time goes.
The air gets colder,
The wind gets louder,
and the sun seems to be darker, at least in my own eyes.

Is it just my psychological effects of gather power, or the objective truth of what's happening?
02
I see the world getting more blue as power is gathered.
Gradually, the gloomy clouds cover the land. The land gets darker without sunshine. Feels like the kind of weather where a bossfight happens in a moment.

I can see people more and more bewildered as my journey continues, but that's out of my scope for my dreams.
